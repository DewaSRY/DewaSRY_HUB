Problem
Dewa needs a centralized platform to build awareness around his products, publish valuable content, and manage users across his SaaS ecosystem. Currently, these responsibilities can become fragmented across different applications, making it difficult to maintain a consistent user experience and centralized view of users, subscriptions, and payments.
Product Purpose
The product will serve as the central platform for the Dewa SaaS ecosystem. It will combine content publishing, user management, SaaS administration, and payment capabilities into a single platform.
The platform will have four primary purposes:
Build Awareness & Content
Publish blogs, articles, tutorials, and product-related content.
Create content that attracts visitors through search engines and other channels.
Introduce users to Dewa's products and services.
Centralized User Hub
Act as the central hub for users of Dewa's SaaS products, including Document Doctor.
Manage authenticated users from connected applications.
Provide a centralized view of users and their relationship with each SaaS product.
SaaS & Subscription Management
Track which users use Dewa's SaaS products.
Manage user subscriptions and product access.
Provide information about the user's current SaaS plan and usage where applicable.
Payment Portal
Integrate with payment gateways to handle SaaS payments.
Allow users to purchase or renew Dewa's SaaS products.
Manage payment status, transactions, and subscription-related payment information.
Ultimately, the platform will become the central hub connecting Dewa's content, users, SaaS products, and payments, while providing a single foundation that can support additional Dewa products in the future.
Who is the user

1. Visitor user
   People who discover your blog, articles, or products but haven't created an account yet.
   They can:
   Read blog articles
   Discover Dewa and dewa Services

2. SaaS Users
   Users who have an account and use Dewa's SaaS products, such as Document Doctor.
   They can:
   Manage their profile
   View subscription status
   View payment history
   Manage their subscription
3. Administrators
   You, as the owner/operator of the platform.
   You can:
   Manage users
   Manage subscriptions
   View payments
   Manage blog articles and image asset
   Manage categories/tags
   Create Articles
   Edit Article
   Publish Article
   Manage Category and tags
